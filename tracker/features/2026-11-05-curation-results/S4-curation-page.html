S4 · The Curation results page

A sixth dashboard page: the headline card with its five states, per-seed table and forest plot, every arm's verdict against the original policy and random removal, family O alongside, and the removed episodes with video.

ACs 11/11 Depends on S1, S2, S3 Data demo is SYNTHETIC; no real Round A yet

In plain words

This is the page a stakeholder opens. It answers one question: does removing the data the ranking called harmful improve the policy? At the top is the spec's headline experiment: curate the dataset once for speed and once for reliability, retrain, and ask whether each curated policy wins on its own metric. The card says in words what was found, the table shows each seed, and the plot shows each policy's advantage with its uncertainty.

Below that is every curated arm judged against the original policy and against random removal of the same number of episodes. Then a short section for the operation-level check (family O), and finally the list of training episodes an arm removed, with video. A red banner says SYNTHETIC on the demo and cannot be switched off. Nothing is corrected across arms or seeds and the page says so.

Terms are defined in the glossary of the main report.

Why

PoC §4.5 calls the head-to-head “the headline result of the PoC”; §2.6 says curated removal must beat random removal; §4.4 asks for the gate first and every claim labelled with metric and τ; §3.4 asks that every number declare what it explains.

“Label every claim with its metric and time cap τ, following PhAIL's disclosure principle.” — §4.4, p. 10

Divergences and limitations

  • Recorded divergence: the forest plot uses mark_rule + mark_point, not mark_errorbar. Measured in headless Chrome: the layered errorbar chart drew a plot area 22 px and 74 px tall despite height=220/270; with rules and points the areas measure 222 and 306 px (check 5.10a). Both are built-in Altair marks; the analyzer accepted this and did not reproduce the 22 px figure itself.
  • Recorded divergence: the page ranks by the all-rollout column on the demo and says “rollouts: all” in the caption (see S3).
  • Recorded divergence: the demo shows only the not-shown state. The other four states are exercised by the S1 fixture; the page has no branch that depends on the state (it prints the recorded status and sentence verbatim), so that is the right place to cover them. The gate-0/3 card is tested on a scratch copy (4.7a).
  • Page checks run only on the demo and scratch copies. The real server summary is first rendered the day Round A finishes.
  • Streamlit's st.dataframe does not wrap text, so the long sentences are shown as text under the arm table, not in cells.
  • Cosmetic: the selected arm's list showed “SYNTHETIC:” twice per line; the refactor removed the duplicate (N6).
