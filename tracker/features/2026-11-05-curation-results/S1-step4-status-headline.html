S1 · Words for each verdict, and the headline state

The step4 summary now records, for every curated arm and metric, a status and a bounded sentence, plus the §4.5 headline state with its sentence.

ACs 7/7 Depends on none Data demo is SYNTHETIC; no real Round A yet

In plain words

When a curated policy is retrained and tested, the test produces numbers per training seed (a seed is one random start, so three seeds show how much is luck). Until now nothing turned those numbers into a plain verdict. This story adds that: for each arm and each metric it says confirmed, refuted, not confirmed, inconclusive, or (for the random-removal controls) no expectation, and writes one careful sentence that always names the metric and its time limit.

It also decides the headline state for the spec's "same dataset, opposite curations" experiment. There are five: shown, partly shown, contradicted, not shown, inconclusive. The analyzer sent the first version back because it said "inconclusive" when the two policies clearly differed but neither win replicated; that case is now "not shown".

Terms are defined in the glossary of the main report.

Why

PoC §4.5 asks for the head-to-head and §4.4 says the gate (did anything change at all?) comes before direction. §9.1 Step 4 asks for confirmation or refutation. None of them says how to combine three seeds or how to word a non-result, so the rule here is the one the repo already used for Round B (vlapoc.confirm.status_of), labelled inferred in the code.

“'Same dataset, opposite curations.' Curate the dataset twice … and show that each curated policy wins on its own metric. … would be the headline result of the PoC.” — §4.5, p. 11

Divergences and limitations

  • The rules are inferred, not specified. The spec gives no across-seed rule and no rule for combining the two claims. The ≥ ⌈2n/3⌉-with-none-contradicting rule is the repo's existing one; the headline rule and where “not shown” begins (gate fired in 2 of 3 seeds) are new and recorded as the rule parameter. Open owner question.
  • The plan contradicted itself on wording (it asked for “no effect” in a sentence while banning that phrase). The executor used “a change that is not detected is not evidence that removal leaves the policy unchanged”. The analyzer accepted it. Reviewer N7 finds that sentence slightly awkward directly after “the distributions differ”; rewording changes recorded strings, so it was left for the owner.
  • Recorded statuses get the SYNTHETIC: prefix only when a select, verify or summary input is synthetic; synthetic train and eval runs are covered by the banner, which walks the whole chain.
  • Summaries recorded before this story lack the k column, so arm labels then read “the top most harmful …”.
