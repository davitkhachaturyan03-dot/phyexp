S1 · Seven corruption transforms

Seven pure functions that turn one clean LIBERO demonstration into a labelled bad one, every action kept inside [−1, 1], each checked on 118 real episodes.

ACs 8/8 Depends on — Code vlapoc/corruptions.py (558 lines)

Why

The PoC's key trick is to plant bad data whose location we know, then ask whether a method finds exactly those episodes. §2.5 lists the corruption types to plant, and §8.10 adds two aimed at specific metrics (idle padding should hurt speed, gripper-release noise should hurt reliability). RoboDrop's appendix fixes the noise level and the shift size. Two owner decisions bind this story: clip every corrupted action to [−1, 1], and build all types except the altered camera viewpoint (that needs simulator re-rendering).

Deliberately corrupt known subsets of training data: noisy or shifted actions, mismatched language instructions, altered camera viewpoints, or low-quality teleoperation demos. Then check whether your attribution method flags exactly those. — §2.1 item 4, p. 3
Action corruption adds zero-mean Gaussian noise with standard deviation 0.25σa … Temporal corruption shifts the action sequence by two seconds relative to the observations. — RoboDrop App. A.1, p. 13

The seven types

Divergences and limitations

  • Action noise touches dims 0–5, not all seven. Clean gripper values are exactly {−1, +1} in 200,340 of 200,340 frames (measured), so noise on the gripper would make values clean data never has. The older v1 noised all seven. Inferred; recorded in each label.
  • Clip leak (measured). Clipping to [−1, 1] is the owner's decision and is satisfied. Clean translation values never exceed ±0.9375, so 1.69% of noised translation values (1.92% in v3's 13 labels) sit in (0.9375, 1], a range clean data never has. A max-absolute-value detector would exploit that (AUROC 0.88 by the analyzer), but smoothness already gives 1.000, so it changes nothing here.
  • RoboDrop does not state the shift direction, a fill, gripper handling or clipping. Direction (actions lead observations) is inferred; the trim over a fill is owner ruling A.
  • detour reverses motion by negating arm deltas (inferred from LIBERO's delta control). It was not replayed in the simulator to confirm the arm really retraces its path.
  • Altered camera viewpoint is out of scope (owner decision 2): it needs re-rendering in the simulator. Owner-approved divergence from §2.1 item 4 and §2.5.
  • Paused and dropped frames repeat stored frames (exact duplicates). That is limitation C of ruling A (see the report).
