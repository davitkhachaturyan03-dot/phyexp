S7 · The verification gate: macro-KS first, then per-metric CIs with Holm

The two-step verdict that counterfactual retraining will be judged by. It first asks whether two policies differ at all (macro-KS), then which way, separately for each metric (episode-clustered bootstrap CIs, Holm across metrics). On PhAIL's real data it says "can't tell" for OpenPI–GR00T and OpenPI–ACT, finds the GR00T–ACT trade-off in 4 of 5 bootstrap seeds, and ranks every model above SmolVLA.

ACs 8/9 met AC7.5 pending server AC7.1 owner-approved divergence (ruling B) Depends on S1, S2, S6

Why

"Two-step verdict per intervention. First the macro-KS gate … Then each metric's bootstrap CI gives the direction for that metric." — PoC §4.4, p. 10
"use the PhAIL significance gate (Section 3.4) to decide whether the change is real before claiming it." — PoC §2.6, p. 5

Two more rules from the same page: crossing curves are the trade-off signature, and four metrics multiply the claims, so "correct for it (e.g. Bonferroni or Holm across metrics)" before labelling a trade-off (quoted, §4.4 p. 10). And from §3.2 p. 6: "KS cannot rank on its own" (quoted).

The PhAIL prose-vs-artifact discrepancy (OpenPI–ACT)

PhAIL p. 9 says "ACT and OpenPI/GR00T are different (KS rejects)" (quoted). PhAIL's own released out/explore/ks_power/dashboard_per_obj_macro.json gives OpenPI–ACT p 0.10294852573713144, status WEAK (measured, analysis-r1). Our gate, on the deterministic sorted export, gives p 0.0960–0.1079 across seeds 0–4 (analysis-r3 N7; the round-1 range 0.105–0.123 came from the unsorted export and is superseded). For GR00T–ACT the released JSON says p 0.016, HOLDS, and our gate fires in 5/5 seeds. PoC §3.3 p. 7's "Macro-KS resolved … OpenPI vs. ACT at 30 rollouts per (model, object) cell" is a detection rate from PhAIL §5.2's subsampling experiment at τ = 120 s, not a full-cohort rejection. The artifact wins: AC7.1 expects (iii) for OpenPI–ACT.

Divergences and limitations

  • AC7.1 GR00T–ACT (ii) in 4/5 seeds at n_boot 1000: owner-approved divergence (2026-10-06, dispute-r2.md). Owner's decision, verbatim: "B. Accept 4/5 (executor)". The analyzer had recommended raising n_boot to 10,000, where the AUC Holm p is 0.025–0.032 and (ii) holds in 5/5 seeds (measured). The ruling keeps n_boot 1000 and records that this trade-off label is not robust to the bootstrap seed at that precision.
  • AC7.5 seed caveat. Under the same ruling the server verdicts keep n_boot 1000. A server verdict whose Holm p lands near α may depend on the bootstrap seed, so the verdict records its seed.
  • gate_only is a fourth outcome PhAIL's taxonomy lacks: the gate fires but no metric's Holm-adjusted CI excludes 0. §4.4 says each metric's CI "gives the direction" (quoted), so none is claimed. It did not occur on PhAIL's pairs. Accepted by the analyzer as a recorded divergence.
  • The human anchor is held fixed in the difference bootstrap (inferred: removes common-mode noise). S2's single-policy HRT CI resamples it, to match PhAIL's Table 2.
  • The p-value floor: with n_boot 1000 the smallest bootstrap p is 2/1001 ≈ 0.002, and Holm × 4 gives 0.008. The macro-KS floor at 2000 draws is 0.0005.
  • Not built: a paired-by-init-state bootstrap (the spec's gate is PhAIL's unpaired pooled resampling; a later refinement). N3: the batch-plan key omits τ_episode, render size and device.
