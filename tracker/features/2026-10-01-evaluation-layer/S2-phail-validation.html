S2 · The metric layer, validated on PhAIL's real-robot data

On PhAIL's own released cohort (990 of its 995 episodes), our metric layer gives the same numbers as PhAIL's own code, to at most 3.6e-15. It reproduces the paper's per-object results exactly and its "four scalars, three winners" table. The p. 8 data question is answered: what is downloadable, under which license, and what is private.

ACs 9/9 met Depends on S1 AC2.3 "incomplete cohort" branch: 23/26 within tolerance

Why

The owner called PhAIL usage "crucial for this POC" (brief, decision 8). The PoC builds its main contribution on the finding that the yardstick picks the winner:

"Four principled scalars on the same data produced three different top-1 models." — PoC §3.3, p. 7
"Confirm which parts of the dataset, including the fine-tuning set, are downloadable and under what license." — PoC §3.5 B, Practical caveats, p. 8

A metric implementation that has never met the data it was designed for is a claim. Reproducing PhAIL's numbers on PhAIL's data turns it into evidence, before anything is computed on LIBERO.

Divergences and limitations

  • The cohort is 990 of 995 episodes. 5 are private (HTTP 403). Every per-object result reproduces exactly; 3 pooled RMST points miss the ±0.1 s tolerance by 0.11–0.60 s. AC2.3 passes under its pre-declared "incomplete" branch, with AC2.1 (implementation equivalence) carrying the pass.
  • PhAIL's HRT-CI producer is not released (plan P19), so the CI variant was chosen by matching endpoints across 8 seeds, not by reproducing PhAIL's draws.
  • Two PhAIL sidecars disagree on pooled RMST (tab_leaderboard.json ACT 100.9451 vs tab_headline_ci.json 100.9287). Ours matches the second, which is Table 2's source. The artifact wins, and this is recorded.
  • PhAIL's build/dashboard.py cannot be imported (it needs build/fig_cdf.py, which is missing from the commit), so its H1 loop is replicated in phail_export_cohort.py around PhAIL's own per_obj_macro_bootstrap_ks.
  • Bootstrap p-values are Monte-Carlo draws, conditional on episode order and seed. PhAIL's loader iterates a Python set, so its order followed hash randomization. Round 2 sorts by s3_url, and every p moved (OpenPI–GR00T 0.1179 → 0.1254; GR00T–ACT 0.0105 → 0.0090). D and the point estimates do not depend on order.
  • The 13.95 GB fine-tuning set was listed, not downloaded: the owner approved only the metadata. The real-robot stage (§3.5 B) is out of scope.
  • pos3 vs our fetcher. The analyzer found pos3 covers --fetch. The stdlib fetcher was kept as an already-executed one-off; pos3 is named as the route to use.
