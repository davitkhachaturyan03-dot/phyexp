S1 · Metric layer: PhAIL's primitives over (T, E) pairs

Every primitive and scalar in PoC §3.2 is now a named function of the Kaplan–Meier CDF. The KM itself comes from PhAIL's own code, vendored byte-identical, so ghost events (T = ∞) and censored timeouts work as PhAIL defines them. One registry names every metric, its functional and its τ.

ACs 7/7 met Depends on — Validated on real data in S2

Why

Owner decision 4 (brief): "cover all metrics the PoC specifies", and where one does not transfer, "say so rather than silently dropping it". The PoC's evaluation layer is PhAIL's distributional method, and every scalar it names is a summary of one CDF:

"Success@τ, median, RMST, UPH, completion, MTBF/A are all projections of F" — PoC §3.2, p. 6

The rule that goes with it, "KS cannot rank on its own" (same page), separates scoring (a scalar with a CI) from significance (macro-KS). S7 builds the verdict on that split.

Example

How ghost events and censoring enter (LABELLED SYNTHETIC, from the re-run): an episode that never succeeds before τ_episode contributes one censored pair, not a failure, and it stays in the risk set.

AC1.4 SYNTHETIC zero-success episode (τ_episode 60 s, 2 ops) -> ((60.0, 0),); all ops done -> ((5.0, 1), (7.0, 1))
      (no censored tail); 1 of 2 done -> ((8.0, 1), (52.0, 0)); LIBERO simultaneous ops (threshold 0) -> ((10.0, 1), (0.0, 1))
AC1.3 SYNTHETIC 55 operations, 5 ghosts (T=inf), 0 censored: ghost_event_rate = 0.09090909090909088, g/n = 0.09090909090909091
AC1.3 RMST(30 s) = 14.55964694266428; mean(min(T, 30)) with ghosts -> 30 = 14.55964694266428; |diff| = 0.0e+00

The asymptote gap 1 − F(∞) is exactly the ghost (hard-failure) rate, as §3.2 says, and RMST integrates a ghost to the cap τ, as PhAIL §3.1 says.

Divergences and limitations

  • Restated, not imported: auc_censored, pp_curve, macro_auc, UPH, MTBF/A and the logrank arithmetic come from PhAIL scripts that import its data loader at module level, so they cannot be imported. Each is restated with its source cited, and S2 shows equality on PhAIL's data (max |diff| 0 to 3.6e-15).
  • macro_ks re-runs the bootstrap loop because PhAIL's _collect_T_E hard-codes its 0.3 s multi-grip threshold (quoted, phail_stats.py:171-178), and LIBERO needs 0. The D and p values are identical to PhAIL's on PhAIL's data.
  • UPH = 3600 / RMST(τ_episode), as in PhAIL's figure code. Table 1's form and common.py's form differ by up to 3× (S2 AC2.8).
  • ghost_event_rate is the KM mass at T = ∞, not the raw g/n (kept as ghost_fraction), because KM moves censored mass to the right.
  • HRT is a ratio (0.1375), where PhAIL prints a percentage (13.75%). mtbfa returns ∞ when F(τ_episode) = 1.
  • "Clustered is always wider" is not general. It holds for positively correlated operations (AC1.5). On LIBERO's human demos the clustered CI of the pooled median was narrower in 6 of 9 two-operation tasks (S4).
