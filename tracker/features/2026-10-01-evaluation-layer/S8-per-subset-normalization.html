S8 · SmolVLA training with per-subset normalization

Every training run now normalizes state and action with statistics computed only from its own training episodes, using LeRobot's own aggregate_stats. Normalization becomes a function of the training data, so a removed episode's effect on the statistics is part of its counterfactual effect. A 2-step SmolVLA fine-tune from smolvla_base proves the hook on this Mac; full training waits for the server.

ACs 4/5 met AC8.5 pending server Depends on S0 (training subsets from blind versions) Owner decision 3 (per-subset)

Why

Owner decision 3 (brief, binding): "Every training run normalizes with mean/std computed only from its own training episodes." Statistics computed over data a run does not train on (corrupted episodes, clean originals or held-out references) would leak into it.

"model, config, training schedule and evaluation protocol are held fixed so that data is the only variable." — PoC, Goal, p. 1

Measured before building: SmolVLA normalizes STATE and ACTION with MEAN_STD, and LeRobot trains with the full version's meta/stats.json even when it is given episodes= (brief). LeRobot's dataset tools already aggregate a subset's stored per-episode stats when they copy a subset (dataset_tools.py:885-950, read). What LeRobot lacks is a way to train on episodes=[…] of an existing dataset with those stats: DatasetConfig has no stats field (measured).

Divergences and limitations

  • AC8.5 fine-tunes from smolvla_base: owner-approved divergence. Round 1 specified LeRobot's LIBERO recipe (--policy.type=smolvla --policy.load_vlm_weights=true), which initializes from the 2.03 GB SmolVLM2 backbone. The owner did not approve that download (brief: approved "the SmolVLA base model"), no PoC page requires the backbone recipe (§2.3 p. 4 says "SmolVLA via LeRobot"), and LeRobot's SmolVLA docs prescribe "Use smolvla_base, our pretrained 450M model, and fine-tune it on your data" (quoted). Steps and batch size come from those docs: 20,000 and 64, "~4 hrs on a single A100 GPU" (quoted).
  • Full SmolVLA training is pending server. Only 2-step local runs exist, as the data+lineage feature's coverage row noted ("SmolVLA not trained yet").
  • Default changed. train_run now normalizes per subset unless told normalization="full". The data+lineage smoke runs were trained with full stats, and their MLflow params say so. The refactorer re-ran the data+lineage dry run under the new default: PASS (N5).
  • Quantile stats are count-weighted means of per-episode quantiles in LeRobot's aggregate_stats, an approximation. ACT and SmolVLA do not use them (MEAN_STD); a QUANTILES-normalized policy would.
  • Cost: each distinct training subset still writes about 7 GB of HF datasets cache (data+lineage R1). The runbook budgets it.
