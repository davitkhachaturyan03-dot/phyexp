S5 · The live local dashboard

A five-page Streamlit app over the lineage store: pick a failed rollout, browse ranked episodes, see clusters and the bad-vs-harmful view, all with video and a provenance banner.

ACs 11/11 Depends on S1, S6 Data scores SYNTHETIC, no real results yet

In plain words

This is the thing a stakeholder opens. It runs on this laptop in a web browser. The first page says what the numbers are. The second lets you pick a failed attempt and see which training recordings pushed it, with video. The third ranks recordings and groups them by task or scene. The fourth shows the groups found in the data. The fifth is the "looks bad but doesn't hurt" view. A red banner on every page says whether the scores are synthetic, smoke or real, and it cannot be switched off in the app.

Terms are defined in the glossary of the main report.

Why

PoC §2.7 specifies the dashboard (both views) and names PhAIL's run explorer as the design reference for the rollout side; §4.5 asks for the heatmap and the throughput-vs-reliability scatter; §3.4 requires each shown number to declare what it explains; §10 lists "dashboard linking failed rollouts to influential episodes".

"or browse a ranked list of harmful episodes, aggregated by any catalog column" — §2.7, p. 5

Divergences and limitations

  • The pages are switched by a sidebar radio, not by a pages/ folder: the analyzer found that a pages/ folder created blank auto-generated pages, so it was renamed views/.
  • "Aggregate by" cannot use the list-valued objects column, so 14 columns are offered. operator and collection_date are empty in this catalog and give one group.
  • AppTest cannot click a table row or chart point; those interactions were verified in a real headless Chrome (--render), and the same state is reachable through a selectbox that AppTest drives. Row-click geometry depends on Streamlit 1.66.1 and should be re-checked after an upgrade.
  • The provenance reader is cached for 15 s, so a re-tagged run can show the old banner for up to 15 s.
  • The real store's only attribute run is a stand-in without declared functionals; the app says it cannot show it instead of showing unlabelled numbers.
