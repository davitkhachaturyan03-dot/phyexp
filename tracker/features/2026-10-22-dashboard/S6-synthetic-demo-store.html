S6 · The synthetic demo store

A demo store the dashboard can show today: real videos, catalog and test attempts with invented scores, plus one real tiny training chain labelled SMOKE.

ACs 5/5 Depends on S1 Data scores SYNTHETIC, no real results yet

In plain words

There are no real attribution scores yet, but the dashboard still has to be shown and tested. So this story builds a store where everything except the scores is real: the training videos and catalog, 20 real test attempts of a real model, and the planted-damage labels. The scores are invented, with known structure planted so that the screens have something to find, and every one is tagged SYNTHETIC. A real two-step training on this Mac is included as well and shows up as SMOKE.

Terms are defined in the glossary of the main report.

Why

Owner answer: real LIBERO videos and catalog, the real (not attribution-grade) smoke run, plus synthetic influence scores clearly marked SYNTHETIC everywhere. PoC §2.7 is what the demo shows.

Divergences and limitations

  • The planted synthetic structure is built from descriptors that also describe the real corruptions, so overlaps with the real planted types appear. They say nothing about attribution (S3).
  • The demo store is not self-contained: runs copied from the real store still read their files from data/lineage/mlartifacts; only new runs write into data/lineage_demo.
  • Only 2 of 20 tasks have rollouts, so the same-task score columns exist for 96 of 960 episodes; the demo pages use the all-rollout columns.
  • NumPy/Accelerate prints spurious floating-point warnings from pre-existing code; the columns are finite.
  • The two partial earlier builds left orphaned untracked data/eval/returns-* folders (gitignored, not deleted).
