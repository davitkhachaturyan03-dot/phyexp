S2 · Clusters stage, three lenses

A tracked stage that finds groups of training recordings that move a metric, in three ways, and writes a plain-language pattern for each, all labelled as estimates.

ACs 9/9 Depends on S1 Data scores SYNTHETIC, no real results yet

In plain words

The owner wanted to pinpoint exact groups of data and see the pattern inside them. The stage looks for groups three ways: by recorded labels (task, scene), by rules over how the recordings behave ("longer than usual for its task AND from scene X"), and by the spec's four groups of how recordings move the five metrics. Each group gets a sentence that says how big it is, which metric it moves and in which direction, and what is different about it. Every group is marked estimated, because adding up individual influence only approximates a group's real effect.

Terms are defined in the glossary of the main report.

Why

PoC §1 names the two kinds of finding stakeholders act on: a metadata group ("demos from operator 3") and a rule over a number ("episodes over 400 steps"). §4.2 gives the four groups.

"Findings such as 'demos from operator 3 hurt drawer tasks' or 'episodes over 400 steps are net harmful' are usually what stakeholders act on." — §1, p. 3

Visual

Power probe (SYNTHETIC, 10 seeds, artifacts/s2_power_probe_SYNTHETIC.json): the planted 98-member slice is in the HRT top 3 in 6 of 10 seeds as a slice (the planted task group in 10 of 10), never a false alarm on F(30) (0 of 10); the 25-member boost slice in 9 of 10; a 37-member slice in 10 of 10. Small slices (about 17–19 members at +1.5 spread) are below the finder's power at the defaults.

Divergences and limitations

  • Slice finding needs ranges, so the bins are our choice (about 192 episodes per quintile on v3); a slice isolates a 13-episode type only through a conjunction. The page says so.
  • The finder also returns large near-population "slices" for noise; the page filters on the estimated reading matching the direction by default.
  • The reading compares a cluster with the subset mean, uncorrected for the number of metrics and clusters. On the server's z-scored ensemble the mean is 0, which removes part of this.
  • On v3 the scene column is partly the suite (480 episodes share one scene value). Operator and date are empty in the LIBERO catalog, so lens 1 is task, scene and suite only.
  • Metric-specific groups are split by (metric, direction) instead of one cluster for the whole group (recorded in the run's parameters).
