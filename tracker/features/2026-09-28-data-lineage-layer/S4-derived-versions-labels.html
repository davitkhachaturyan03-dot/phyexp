S4 · Derived dataset versions with ground-truth labels: the injection hook

A corrupted copy of the dataset is now a new version whose parentage is recorded. Only the edited episodes change hash, and what was done to them lives in a separate labels.parquet that the method under test never reads. The demo derivation v1 differs from v0 in exactly 100 episode versions; the other 900 are identical.

ACs 8/8 met Depends on S3 v1 16076b9c5cb2… · data/lerobot/libero_spatial_10__v1/

Why

The PoC proves an attribution method by planting bad episodes and checking whether the method finds them. That needs a record of exactly which episode versions were corrupted and how, kept away from the method, plus references that are never touched.

"Deliberately corrupt known subsets of training data … Then check whether your attribution method flags exactly those." — §2.1 item 4, p. 3
Ground truth: "Injection experiment with 5–10% corrupted episodes, using LIBERO's original demos as clean references" — §10, p. 20
"Corrupt episodes two ways: pad some with idle or hesitant segments …" — §8.10, p. 17 (so edits may change length)

The one demo corruption follows RoboDrop App. A.1 (p. 13) literally: "Action corruption adds zero-mean Gaussian noise with standard deviation 0.25σa to the demonstrated actions" (quoted). The full §2.5 corruption suite is a separate later feature: owner-approved scope (brief.md, 2026-09-29).

Divergences and limitations

  • Round 1 leaked the ground truth into the method-facing table. The executor's first derive_version also wrote corruption_type into v1's episode table, which every downstream stage reads. The analyzer caught it (analysis-r1 E2), and round 2 removed it. Both v1 tables were rewritten in place (sha256 135c0655… → 3fcca3a4…); the v1 id, the 100/100 labels join and the 100/900 diff are unchanged.
  • The rewrite left no trace in the lineage store. No episode-table digest exists to record it (see S3). A stale copy with the column remains in the round-1 scratch root data/lineage_scratch/s5/; no library path reads it.
  • Noised actions leave LIBERO's Box(−1, 1) (clip: null): 51% of corrupted frames have a dimension outside [−1, 1], and the gripper ranges −2.07 to +1.96. The gripper stops being ±1, so gripper_toggles is meaningless on corrupted episodes (84–428 vs 1–5). Whether to clip is for the injection feature.
  • All 7 dims are noised, the gripper included. RoboDrop doesn't say; this is the literal reading, recorded in params.dims.
  • Edited episodes move position. They are appended, so in v1 they are episode_index 900–999. Identity travels with episode_id.
  • LeRobot's task_index order in a derived dataset depends on PYTHONHASHSEED (dataset_tools.py:549, list(set(tasks)); refactorer, measured). Content hashes and version ids are unaffected. files.json digests and the task_index stats of a re-derivation will differ.
  • Cost: a derivation takes about 5 min and writes a full 6.8 GB copy (delete_episodes' metadata loop alone is 166–181 s), plus a temporary cache that is now deleted.
  • Ground truth can be reconstructed from derivation.json (selection rule plus seed). That file sits beside labels.parquet, not in the catalog. The method should read episodes only through version_episodes / Subset.
